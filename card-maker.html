This page draws the large preview picture that Facebook shows for each article.

  1. Wait a few seconds until both pictures appear below.
  2. Check the list under “Photos found”. If any photo shows a red ✖, choose that photo from your phone using the buttons on this page.
  3. Tap Download under each picture. (If the download doesn’t start, press and hold the picture and choose Save.)
  4. Upload the two downloaded pictures to your GitHub repository, next to index.html.
