After the Flood, What Should Farmers Plant?

Agricultural recovery after a disaster is not simply about replacing the crops that were lost. It also raises an important question: what should farmers plant next?


Crop and varietal diversity can provide farmers with different options for managing climate risks, markets and changing production conditions.


This article explores agricultural recovery through the lens of crop diversity, considering farmer profitability, agronomic suitability, seed systems, markets, infrastructure and climate resilience in Nepal.
